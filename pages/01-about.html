about

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Houston. My research is in applied economics, with a particular interest in evidence based approaches to study policies that addresses social and development issues. I draw my motivation from questions in development, labor and public economics using theory and observation.

I was born and raised in Kolkata, India. I moved to Bhopal, India where I completed my bachelors with masters. I currently live in Houston, USA.
