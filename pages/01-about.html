about

I am an Applied Economist. My research uses evidence-based approaches to study and inform policies, so as to address socioeconomic and development challenges. I draw my motivation from questions in Labor, Public and Development Economics, using data for observation and theory for models.

I was born and raised in Kolkata, India, then moved to Bhopal, India, where I completed my bachelors with masters in economics at IISER Bhopal. Currently I am a PhD student in economics at the University of Houston, USA.
